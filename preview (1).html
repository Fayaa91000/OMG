Welcome to OMG Miner

This guide explains the proposed OMG Miner setup process, wallet requirements, mining schedule and Gaza Sadaqah agreement.

Only install software from the official OMG release source. Never install an unknown mining executable or share your wallet recovery phrase.

Step 1 – Create a Monero Wallet

OMG is designed around Monero (XMR) mining. You need a compatible Monero wallet to receive your mining payouts.

Official Monero downloads:
https://www.getmonero.org/downloads/

Download the official wallet from the Monero website and follow its instructions to create a wallet.

Step 2 – Protect Your Wallet

  • Keep your recovery phrase offline and private.
  • Never send your seed phrase to OMG or anyone else.
  • Never share your private keys or wallet password.
  • Use your public receiving address when configuring mining payouts.
OMG should only require your public wallet address for payout configuration. Never enter your wallet recovery phrase into mining software.

Step 3 – Configure Your Mining Settings

A. Open the official OMG Miner application.
B. Enter your Monero public receiving address.
C. Select an appropriate mining thread count for your hardware.
D. Review the pool configuration and any applicable pool fees.

The available pool, connection address and dashboard must be supplied by the actual configured mining pool. Do not use an unverified pool address.

Step 4 – Understand the Four-Hour Mining Cycle

First session: 3 hours

Personal mining session for the user.

Second session: 1 hour

Gaza Sadaqah mining session, after the required user agreement.

Cycle completion

After the three-hour personal session and one-hour Gaza session, the four-hour cycle ends and mining stops.

New cycle rule: If you want to mine again, OMG must show the Gaza agreement again. You must actively accept it before another cycle begins. No automatic restart without consent.

Step 5 – Reward Allocation

The proposed gross mining reward allocation is:

  • 75% – User
  • 20% – Gaza Sadaqah
  • 5% – OMG software and operating costs

These percentages add up to 100%. The Gaza share is not an additional deduction on top of the one-hour session. The reward allocation and mining schedule are separate parts of the system.

Pool fees and network transaction fees, if applicable, must be disclosed separately.

Step 6 – Check Your Mining Balance

Use the official dashboard of the mining pool configured by OMG to check accepted shares, estimated rewards and payout status.

Your wallet balance may not update immediately. Mining pools may require a minimum payout threshold and confirmations before funds appear in your wallet.

A verified pool dashboard link will be added when the actual pool configuration is finalized.

Step 7 – Windows Security and Installation

Windows may display a warning when an application is downloaded from an unfamiliar publisher. Some mining programs may also be flagged by antivirus products because miners can be abused by malware.

Do not disable Microsoft Defender, Smart App Control or other security protections just to install OMG. Do not create broad antivirus exclusions.
  • Download only from the official OMG release.
  • Check the publisher signature and published checksum when available.
  • Verify the source code and release before running the software.
  • Investigate antivirus detections rather than automatically ignoring them.
  • Do not run software you cannot verify.

Important Security Commitments

  • OMG should never request your wallet seed phrase.
  • OMG should never request your private keys or wallet password.
  • No hidden mining sessions.
  • No undisclosed OMG allocation or hidden fees.
  • Your hardware belongs to you.
  • You are responsible for your electricity costs.
  • A new mining cycle requires fresh agreement.

Before You Start

Make sure you understand:

  • The risks and costs of cryptocurrency mining.
  • The four-hour mining schedule.
  • The Gaza Sadaqah agreement.
  • The 75% / 20% / 5% reward allocation.
  • Your wallet and software security responsibilities.

Mining rewards are not guaranteed. Actual results depend on hardware, electricity costs, pool conditions, network difficulty and other factors.

OMG aims to combine transparent mining, user control and a dedicated Sadaqah allocation. Thank you for taking the time to understand the process before participating.
