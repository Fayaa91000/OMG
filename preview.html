Our Gaza Sadaqah Commitment

OMG Miner is designed to give users an opportunity to contribute a portion of their mining rewards towards a dedicated Gaza Sadaqah allocation.

Our objective is to make the contribution process understandable, visible and transparent. Users should know how the allocation works before they begin mining.

01. The Four-Hour Mining Cycle

3 Hours Personal mining session
1 Hour Gaza Sadaqah mining session

How the cycle works

  1. The user starts a personal mining session for three hours.
  2. The user then enters the one-hour Gaza Sadaqah mining session.
  3. After the four-hour cycle is completed, mining stops.
  4. If the user wants to mine again, OMG asks for a fresh agreement to the Gaza session.
Important: The software must not silently start another mining cycle. A new cycle requires the user's agreement again.

02. Mining Reward Allocation

The proposed allocation of gross mining rewards is:

RecipientAllocation
User mining rewards75%
Gaza Sadaqah allocation20%
OMG software and operating costs5%
Total100%
The 20% Gaza allocation is the Gaza share of mining rewards. The one-hour Gaza session is part of the mining schedule, not an extra 20% deduction. Rewards must never be counted twice.

Any applicable mining pool fees or network transaction fees must be disclosed separately before mining or payout.

03. User Agreement

Before starting a new mining cycle, users must be shown the mining schedule and reward allocation and must actively agree.

  • Three hours of personal mining.
  • One hour of Gaza Sadaqah mining.
  • The published 75% / 20% / 5% reward allocation.
  • Mining stops when the four-hour cycle is complete.
  • Fresh agreement is required before another cycle.

Users who do not accept the agreement cannot start that mining cycle.

04. Transparency and Distribution

OMG aims to provide clear records of mining rewards, allocation calculations, applicable fees and Gaza distributions.

The Gaza allocation should be supported by verifiable transaction records and documented distribution information. A wallet transfer alone does not prove that funds reached intended beneficiaries.

No donation or distribution should be described as completed until the transfer and relevant delivery evidence have been verified.

05. Your Hardware, Your Electricity

  • You use your own mining hardware.
  • You are responsible for your own electricity costs.
  • Mining performance depends on your hardware, network and pool conditions.
  • Mining rewards are not guaranteed.

06. Security and User Protection

  • OMG must never request your wallet recovery phrase or seed phrase.
  • OMG must never request your private keys or wallet password.
  • Never share your wallet security details with anyone.
  • Use only official OMG software releases.
  • Verify software downloads before installing.
Security reminder: Your public wallet address is not your recovery phrase. Never give anyone your seed phrase or private key.

07. No Hidden Fees. No Hidden Mining.

OMG's commitment is to disclose its allocation and operating costs clearly. The proposed 5% OMG allocation is included in the 100% reward split.

  • No undisclosed OMG allocation.
  • No undisclosed mining activity.
  • No mining cycle restarted without user agreement.
  • No additional fee presented as part of the published allocation without prior disclosure.

Our Promise

We believe that technology should be used responsibly. OMG's goal is to make mining understandable while creating a transparent pathway for a dedicated Gaza Sadaqah contribution.

Transparency matters. Consent matters. Every contribution matters.
