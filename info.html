Mining with Purpose.
Contributing with Heart.

OMG introduces a voluntary Sadaqah mining allocation model. Use your computing power for your own mining and dedicate a portion of your mining time to humanitarian support for Gaza.

How the Sadaqah Allocation Works

A proposed four-hour mining cycle, with clear time allocation.

3 HOURS Personal Mining Your mining rewards go to your configured personal wallet.
1 HOUR Gaza Sadaqah Mining rewards are directed to the designated Gaza allocation wallet.
03

Your Personal Mining

You dedicate three hours to your own mining activity and your configured wallet.

01

Gaza Sadaqah Mining

You voluntarily dedicate one hour of computing power to the Gaza allocation.

Allocation completion matters: If the Gaza allocation target is not reached during a one-hour session, the progress remains visible. Additional contribution sessions can be offered until the target is reached, but mining must never continue beyond the user's consent or selected limits.

Your Hardware. Your Decision.

OMG is designed around user control and voluntary participation.

01 — Your Hardware

You provide and control your own computer or mining hardware.

02 — Your Electricity

Mining consumes electricity. You are responsible for your power costs and should understand them before participating.

03 — Your Permission

You decide whether to participate, when mining starts, and when it stops.

OMG Transparency & Security Promise

Your security is more important than your mining rewards.

🛡

OMG NEVER REQUESTS YOUR WALLET PHRASE

OMG will never ask you to provide your wallet recovery phrase, seed phrase, private keys, or passwords. Never share these security details with anyone.

✓

NO HIDDEN FEES

OMG does not charge undisclosed platform fees. Applicable pool, network, or transaction fees must be clearly explained.

⚙

NO HIDDEN MINING

Mining must only operate with your knowledge and permission. You should always be able to see and control mining activity.

⚡

YOUR HARDWARE, YOUR POWER

You own your hardware and pay for the electricity it uses. OMG must be transparent about resource usage.

♥

VOLUNTARY SADAQAH

Your contribution is a personal choice. You should know the destination wallet and the allocation rules before participating.

Transparency: Every Contribution Matters

The platform should provide a verifiable allocation record.

Mining Time

Record personal and Gaza mining hours separately.

Actual Rewards

Show the rewards actually earned, not estimated promises.

Fees

Disclose applicable pool, network, and transaction fees.

Wallet Transfers

Show actual transfers and transaction references when available.

Allocation Target

Display the target, amount received, and remaining balance.

Distribution Records

Provide evidence of onward distribution when available and independently verifiable.

Wallet receipts and mining records alone do not prove that aid has reached individual beneficiaries. OMG should publish verifiable distribution information and clearly identify the organization responsible for handling the funds.
